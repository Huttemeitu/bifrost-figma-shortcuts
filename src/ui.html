Turn off what you never use, so the palette only shows what matters. A group's checkbox hides or shows everything in it; open a group to pick single values.

Aliases are your own shortcuts, like cta for f brand p m. Use them anywhere a command can start: cta r l.

Words or numbers work as names, e.g. 1 for your most used combo.
